## Description

Notch turns the notch on your MacBook into your day at a glance.

Beside the camera, without opening anything: what's up next on the left, what's happening now on
the right. Rest the pointer on the notch and it opens: the task you're doing, with Done and Focus
right there, and everything ahead — meetings and tasks — in one list.

FOCUS
• Pomodoro rounds with breaks that start by themselves
• Focus mode: a large countdown, the task you're on, and nothing else competing
• Reminders wait until your break; meeting warnings still come through
• Focus Guard (optional) nudges you back when a game or video app runs long

PLAN
• Each morning, pick up to three tasks against the time your calendar actually leaves
• A 20% buffer warning when the plan is more than the day holds
• An evening wrap-up, with one line for tomorrow

ORGANIZE
• A board: To Do, Doing, Done
• Projects, subtasks, deadlines, reminders, repeats, priority, tags
• Dependencies: a task stays locked until what it waits on is done

BANNERS FROM THE NOTCH
Reminders, deadlines, meetings about to start, and the end of a focus round drop down from the
notch, then tuck back.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no network. Your tasks and plans stay on your Mac.

Works best on a MacBook with a notch; on other Macs it draws a small stand-in at the top of the
screen. Requires macOS 14 or later.

## Notes for App Review

Notch lives in the menu bar area around the MacBook's camera housing.
- Rest the pointer on the notch (the black area at the top center of the screen) to open it;
  move away to close it. On a Mac without a notch, a small black bar at the top center stands in.
- Calendar access is optional; without it, Notch still shows tasks, the planner and the timer.
- To see a banner quickly: open the notch, click "All tasks", add a task, select it, turn on
  "Remind me" and set it one minute ahead. When it rings, a banner drops from the notch with
  Snooze and Done.
- The full window: open the notch and click "All tasks".
- Focus Guard is off by default (right-click the open notch › Focus Guard). In this edition it
  checks only the name of the app in front; it does not read browsers.
- Open at Login is off by default and only turns on when the user chooses it from the same menu.
- The app makes no network requests and collects no data.
