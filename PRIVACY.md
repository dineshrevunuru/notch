# Notch privacy policy

_Last updated: November 19, 2026_

**Notch collects no data.** It has no account, no analytics, no tracking, and makes no network
requests. Everything it knows stays on your Mac.

## What Notch keeps, and where

Your tasks, projects, day plans, focus time and settings are saved in Notch's own folder on your
Mac. In the Mac App Store edition, that's the app's sandbox container. Nothing is sent anywhere.
Deleting Notch deletes them.

## Calendar

If you allow it, Notch reads the events in the macOS Calendar app, to show what's next and how
much of your day is free. It never changes or shares your events. You can turn this off in
System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars.

## Focus Guard

Focus Guard is off unless you turn it on. When it's on, during your workday or a focus round, it
checks which app is in front, so it can nudge you when a game or video app has run long.

- **Mac App Store edition:** it looks only at the name of the app in front.
- **Direct-download edition:** it can also read the address of the active tab in Chrome or Safari,
  if you allow it, to recognize sites like YouTube.

It keeps only the name (for example "YouTube") and the minutes, on your Mac, for your evening
wrap-up. Addresses are never stored.
