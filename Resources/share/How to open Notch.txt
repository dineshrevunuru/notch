Notch — your notch, as a planner

INSTALL
1. Double-click the Notch zip file. Notch appears next to it.
2. Drag Notch into your Applications folder.
3. Open Notch from Applications.

THE FIRST TIME IT OPENS
macOS will say it can't check Notch for malicious software. That's because this
copy isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate yet. To open it anyway:
  1. Click Done (or Cancel) on that message.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to Security, find "Notch was blocked to protect your Mac",
     and click "Open Anyway".
  4. Click Open Anyway again, and enter your Mac password if asked.
You only do this once.

Then macOS asks if Notch may see your calendar. Allow it to see your meetings
in the notch. Everything stays on your Mac.

USING IT
- Rest your pointer on the notch at the top of your screen to open it.
- Beside the camera: what's up next (left) and what's happening now (right).
- "All tasks" or "Plan your day" opens the full window: board, planner, lists.
- Start a Pomodoro focus round with the Focus button.
- Right-click empty space in the open notch for Focus Guard, Open at Login, and Quit.

NEEDS
macOS 14 or later. Best on a MacBook with a notch; on other Macs it draws a small
stand-in at the top of the screen.
